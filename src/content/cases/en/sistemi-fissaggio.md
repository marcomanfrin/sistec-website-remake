## The challenge

The traditional production model struggled to keep pace with the variety of the product range.

- **Managing variety**: different tube sizes, shapes and flange types to process.
- **Setup times**: conventional mechanical systems required long machine downtime at every product change.
- **Process efficiency**: cutting, drilling, threading and welding were hard to balance in a single continuous flow.

## The solution

The system is built on an architecture of four articulated robots that integrate every process stage.

- **Integrated process**: the 6-metre tube is automatically cut to length, punched and threaded.
- **Robotic welding**: the stamped flanges are joined by robot-controlled spot welding, for consistent precision.
- **Machine vision**: part recognition and positioning ensure maximum loading flexibility.
- **Batch-one production**: the management software switches from one format to another instantly, making even the smallest batches efficient.

## The results

- **Optimised cycle**: a balanced workload across the robots maximises output and reduces idle time.
- **Total flexibility**: real-time response to market demand, even for small, diversified batches.
- **Quality and repeatability**: millimetre precision in CNC machining and welding, with a dramatic reduction in scrap.
- **Modularity**: the system adapts quickly to new product variants with no structural changes.
