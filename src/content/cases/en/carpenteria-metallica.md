## The challenge

In complex electrical enclosure fabrication, the traditional model suffered from fragmented process stages.

- **Multi-material processing**: mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium alloys all had to be processed with the same efficiency.
- **Lead times**: manual transfers between welding, stud fixing and grinding created bottlenecks and increased the margin for error.
- **Cosmetic finish**: achieving uniform grinding without skilled operators was difficult.

## The solution

A flexible robotic cell combining state-of-the-art joining and finishing technologies.

- **Laser, MIG and CMT welding**: low heat input processes ensure maximum structural precision and reduce distortion on thin materials.
- **Stud welding**: fast fixing of studs and inserts, with millimetre-accurate positioning handled by the robot.
- **Grinding**: an integrated finishing station removes imperfections and leaves the product ready for subsequent treatments, with no manual intervention.
- **Pallet handling**: intelligent load management ensures non-stop productivity and optimised internal logistics.

## The results

The move to an integrated production model has delivered a measurable competitive advantage.

- **Millimetre precision**: full control over welding and grinding, with a dramatic reduction in scrap and rework.
- **Streamlined flow**: synchronising the four technologies eliminates the bottlenecks typical of traditional metal fabrication.
- **Real-time response**: small batches and prototypes are produced with the same efficiency as series production.
