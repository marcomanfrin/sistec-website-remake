## The challenge

The project involved technical complexities that directly affected productivity.

- **Extreme variability**: the many small parts had variable positions, making dedicated clamping jigs impossible.
- **Critical dimensions**: workpieces up to 3.5 metres long required a wide working envelope.
- **High idle times**: loading and clamping heavy, bulky structures took up an excessive share of the cycle time.
- **Loading logistics**: structural parts had to be moved by overhead crane in an area crowded with track-mounted robots.

## The solution

We designed the system architecture to maximise overlapped time and operational flexibility.

- **Three track-mounted robots**: two KUKA IONTEC robots dedicated to welding and a third high-accuracy (HA) KUKA robot to handle and position parts in real time.
- **Robot-as-jig technology**: the handling robot positions small parts with millimetre precision while the welding robot tack-welds them, eliminating the cost and footprint of hundreds of fixed jigs.
- **Dual mobile positioner station**: two welding fixtures on separate shuttle carriages move the loading area outside the robots’ working envelope, so the overhead crane can be used in complete safety.
- **Self-adjusting fixtures**: the robot automatically adjusts the fixture length by moving its mobile parts along recirculating ball guides, instantly adapting to the model being welded.
- **Automatic tool change**: a dedicated tool storage and electro-pneumatic changers on the robot wrist manage different gripping systems.

## The results

- **Shorter cycle time**: two robots weld the same part simultaneously, taking advantage of the workpiece length.
- **Overlapped time**: while one side of the system is welding, the operator loads structural parts onto the second carriage, eliminating machine downtime.
- **Total model flexibility**: the system reconfigures itself for lengths from 2,000 to 3,500 mm, with no manual intervention on the tooling.
- **Optimised space**: the shuttle carriages resolve the conflict between the robots’ footprint and heavy-load crane manoeuvres.

## Technical specifications

- **Welding**: 2 × KUKA KR20 R3100 IONTEC on a linear track, for maximum operating coverage.
- **Handling**: 1 × KUKA KR120 R3100-2 HA (High Accuracy) on a linear track, optimised for millimetre-accurate positioning.
- **Dimensional capacity**: workpieces with a cross-section of up to 1000 × 600 mm and a length of up to 3,500 mm.
- **Process integration**: welding packages mounted on the tracks, for maximum freedom of movement and no interference during the most complex operations.
