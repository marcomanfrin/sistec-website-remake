## The challenge

Traditional sheet metal component production often suffers from inefficiencies caused by fragmented processes.

- **Manual handling**: semi-finished parts move between isolated machines, creating idle time and risks for the operator.
- **Technology synchronisation**: coordinating punching machines and benders from different manufacturers in a single smooth cycle is complex.
- **Precision and repeatability**: holding millimetre tolerances on high volumes without manual positioning fixtures is difficult.

## The solution

We built a robotic architecture that ensures maximum synergy between the technologies involved.

- **End-to-end process**: the system autonomously picks the sheet from the stack and returns the finished part, punched, bent and fitted with inserts.
- **Multi-vendor integration**: a LAG Rainer punching machine and a Schröder panel bender work together, coordinated by centralised robotic logistics.
- **Intelligent handling**: articulated robots manage loading, unloading and ultra-precise positioning during the bending and staking stages.
- **Flexible automation**: the management software optimises the entire cycle and makes even small, variable batch production efficient.

## The results

- **Autonomy and safety**: material handling is fully automated, with no heavy lifting for the operator and dramatically reduced idle time.
- **Streamlined flow**: a perfectly balanced cycle between punching and bending eliminates bottlenecks.
- **Consistent quality**: millimetre precision at every stage, thanks to repeatable robotic control.
- **Modularity and flexibility**: the system adapts quickly to new product variants with no structural changes.
