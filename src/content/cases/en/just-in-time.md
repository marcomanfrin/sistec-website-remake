## The challenge

The traditional production model had a number of structural weaknesses that limited the company’s responsiveness.

- **Inefficient small batches**: manual handling made producing small quantities uneconomical.
- **Tied-up capital**: fast delivery depended on stock, with high warehousing costs.
- **No compromise on finish**: in designer furniture, imperfections are not acceptable, and parts must be ready for critical surface treatments such as chrome plating.

## The solution

We developed an infrastructure based on four independent robotic cells, designed to work in synergy and deliver maximum flexibility.

- **Integrated process**: full automation from forming the metal profile, tube and wire up to 12 mm, through to final welding, reducing the variables associated with manual work.
- **Welding under control**: systems with dynamic parameter adjustment ensure uniform, consistent weld beads.
- **Batch-one production**: integration with the company MES makes it possible to produce exactly what is needed in real time, so even the smallest batches are viable.

## The results

An Industry 4.0 approach has taken the company to new levels of efficiency.

- **Production capacity**: around 7,000 parts a month on a single shift.
- **Optimised cash flow**: the move to Just-in-Time has dramatically reduced warehousing requirements.
- **Quality and precision**: the millimetre repeatability of the robotic processes has all but eliminated scrap.
- **Modularity**: the system is already prepared for future developments, such as integrating AGVs for autonomous material handling.

## Technical architecture and performance

At the heart of the system are four robotic cells that can operate both fully independently and in perfect synergy. The configuration handles metal tube and wire up to 12 mm with great flexibility, delivering flawless results for the most demanding markets. This positions the company as the ideal partner for designer and international contract furniture, where speed of response and attention to detail are non-negotiable.
