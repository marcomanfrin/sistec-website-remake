## The context

This technological evolution turned a historically critical stage into a competitive advantage, making it possible to:

- **Enable 24/7 production**: thanks to automation, the company has successfully introduced an unmanned night shift.
- **Eliminate cosmetic rejects**: robotic handling preserves component integrity, which is essential when design and surface finish are top priorities.
- **Streamline flows**: moving directly from cutting to part separation has eliminated bottlenecks, raising the efficiency standard of the entire plant.

## The challenge

For a brand whose hallmark is flawless AISI 304 stainless steel surfaces, the challenges were ambitious.

- **Protecting cosmetic quality**: manual handling caused micro-scratches and defects, leading to costly rejects.
- **Fully utilising the equipment**: more production capacity was needed through night shifts, without increasing fixed labour costs.
- **Logistical complexity**: highly varied nests, with small batches of different products on the same sheet, made manual sorting slow and error-prone.

## The solution

We designed a smart manufacturing cell that does more than cut: it manages the entire flow of semi-finished parts.

- **Intelligent robotic sorting**: a KUKA KR Quantec robot picks the cut parts one by one and sorts them with millimetre precision onto 24 different pallets.
- **Automatic gripper management**: the system selects and configures the gripper best suited to each part’s geometry on its own, working in overlapped time.
- **Machine vision for zero rejects**: an integrated camera monitors the skeleton and decides how to handle unloading, for a fully reliable night cycle.
- **Integrated part flipper**: parts are flipped and stacked with the burr facing the right way, simplifying subsequent deburring.

## The results

The engineering approach shifted the focus from pure speed to overall process efficiency.

- **Unmanned production**: a reliable night shift that dramatically increases daily output.
- **Rejects eliminated**: no more drag scratches, protecting the value of premium materials.
- **Just-in-Time flexibility**: small batches and complex nests handled with the same efficiency as large production runs.
- **People empowered**: staff have been freed from repetitive sorting tasks and redeployed to higher value-added stages.

## Technical architecture and performance

- **Integrated configuration**: native integration between TRUMPF laser cutting, a KUKA KR Quantec handling robot and a high-speed Cartesian system.
- **Logistics capacity**: 24 automatic palletising stations to handle a wide range of part codes and batches simultaneously.
- **Smart handling**: an automatic gripper store allows the system to reconfigure itself in real time for any geometry, with no manual intervention.
- **Optimised downstream processes**: the flipping system correctly orients semi-finished parts and simplifies deburring and finishing.
