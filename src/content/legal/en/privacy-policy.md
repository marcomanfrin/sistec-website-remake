This page describes how the personal data of users who visit the website is processed. This notice is provided pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”) and applicable national legislation to anyone who interacts with the web services of SISTEC AM SRL, available at [www.sistec-am.com](https://www.sistec-am.com/).

This notice applies only to the SISTEC AM SRL website and its subdomains, and not to any other websites that may be visited via links on this site.

This notice is also based on Recommendation no. 2/2001, adopted on 17 May 2001 by the European data protection authorities meeting in the Working Party established by Article 29 of Directive 95/46/EC, which sets out the minimum requirements for collecting personal data online: in particular, the manner, timing and nature of the information that controllers must provide to users when they connect to web pages, regardless of the purpose of the connection.

## Who processes personal data

The Data Controller, i.e. the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing, is **SISTEC AM SRL Unipersonale**, with registered office at Viale Trento 105/M, 33077 Sacile (PN), Italy.

## Types of data processed, purposes and legal basis

### Browsing data

The IT systems and software procedures that run this website acquire, in the course of their normal operation, certain personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols. This information is not collected in order to be associated with identified data subjects, but by its very nature could, through processing and association with data held by third parties, allow users to be identified.

This category includes the IP addresses or domain names of the devices used, the URIs of the resources requested, the time of the request, the method used to submit it to the server, the size of the file obtained in response, the numerical code indicating the status of the server’s response, and other parameters relating to the user’s operating system and IT environment.

This data is used solely to obtain anonymous statistical information on the use of the website and to check that it is working correctly, and is deleted after processing. It may be used to establish liability in the event of any computer crimes committed against the website.

### Data provided voluntarily by the user

The optional, explicit and voluntary sending of messages to the addresses indicated on the website, including via the forms on its pages, involves the acquisition of the sender’s address, which is needed to reply, and of any other personal data included in the message.

Data provided voluntarily is processed for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

| Purpose | Legal basis (Article 6 GDPR) |
| --- | --- |
| Responding to requests for information on the services, products and solutions of SISTEC AM SRL, and handling reports of any kind. | User request, made by sending a message, including via the contact form. |
| Managing job applications (e-recruitment) and other forms of professional collaboration, whether speculative or submitted via the dedicated form. | User request, made by submitting an application. |
| Subscription to the newsletter service with informational and promotional content. | User consent, given by subscribing to the service. |

To stop receiving the newsletter, simply click the link at the bottom of any email or write to [sistec@sistec-am.com](mailto:sistec@sistec-am.com).

Any specific notices will be shown or displayed on the pages of the website dedicated to particular services.

## Provision of data

Apart from browsing data, as specified above, the provision of data is optional. However, failure to provide it may make it impossible for SISTEC AM SRL to provide the requested services.

Consent, where required for specific purposes, is always optional and may be withdrawn at any time by writing to [sistec@sistec-am.com](mailto:sistec@sistec-am.com).

## Disclosure of data

Without prejudice to legal obligations, the data collected may be disclosed to:

- parties to whom disclosure is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party, for pre-contractual measures taken at their request or, in general, for the purposes set out in this notice;
- parties that process data on behalf of the Controller as Processors pursuant to Article 28 GDPR, such as providers of IT system and telecommunications network management services (including email). The complete, up-to-date list of Processors is available on request from the Controller’s registered office;
- parties authorised to access the data under applicable law, or to whom the data must be disclosed to comply with legal obligations.

The data may also be processed by employees and collaborators of the Controller’s relevant departments, who are expressly authorised to process it pursuant to Article 29 GDPR and applicable national legislation.

## Transfer of data abroad

Data is not transferred outside the European Union. Any use of technological solutions involving a transfer to third countries will take place exclusively in compliance with Articles 44 et seq. of the GDPR: where there is an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, provided that data subjects have enforceable rights and effective legal remedies, or in the cases of derogation provided for by law.

## Retention periods

Data is kept for no longer than is necessary to achieve the purposes indicated. In particular:

- data provided voluntarily by the user: until the request has been dealt with, or in accordance with the time limits provided for by law;
- data processed for sending the newsletter: 24 months;
- data processed for informational, promotional and commercial purposes: 24 months;
- data needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim may be kept for longer, in line with the limitation periods provided for by law.

Whether the data retained is still relevant to the purposes for which it was collected is checked periodically.

## Data subject rights

Data subjects have the right, where applicable, to obtain access to their personal data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of processing, or to object to processing (Articles 15 et seq. GDPR), by writing to the Controller at [sistec@sistec-am.com](mailto:sistec@sistec-am.com).

## Right to lodge a complaint

Data subjects who believe that the processing of their data infringes the GDPR have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of the Member State in which they habitually reside, work, or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR), or to bring proceedings before the competent courts.
