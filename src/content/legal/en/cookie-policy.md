## What cookies are

Cookies are small text strings that the websites you visit save on your device. They can serve several purposes: letting you move efficiently between pages, remembering your preferences and, in general, improving your experience on the website. Similar technologies, such as the browser’s local storage, belong to the same category.

- **Technical cookies**: these are needed for the website to work or to provide a service you have explicitly requested, such as remembering your cookie choices. They do not require consent.
- **Analytics cookies**: these collect information, in aggregate form, on the number of users and how they visit the website, in order to produce statistics on the service.
- **Profiling or marketing cookies**: these may be used to track browsing and consumer habits, including for the purpose of showing personalised advertising.
- **Third-party cookies**: these are set by domains other than the one you are visiting, for example by an embedded video.

To find out more, you can visit [allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org), [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu) and the dedicated page of the Italian Data Protection Authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/cookie).

## How consent works on this website

On your first visit, a banner lets you **accept**, **reject** or **customise** the use of non-technical cookies. Until you make a choice, only technical tools are active on the website: no analytics or third-party cookies are set and no external content is loaded.

- Google Analytics is activated only if you consent to the **Analytics** category.
- YouTube videos are loaded only if you consent to the **Media content** category, or when you choose to play an individual video. Until then, thumbnails are served directly from our own server.

Your choice is remembered for 6 months. You can change it at any time using the **Cookie preferences** button at the bottom of every page, or here:

## How to disable cookies in your browser

In addition to the preferences panel, you can manage or delete cookies from your browser settings:

- **Microsoft Edge**: three-dot menu at the top right › Settings › Privacy, search and services › Cookies and site permissions.
- **Google Chrome**: three-dot menu at the top right › Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies.
- **Mozilla Firefox**: three-line menu at the top right › Settings › Privacy & Security.
- **Safari**: Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data.
