We are looking for an Electrical Design Engineer to join our engineering team and develop innovative industrial automation and robotics solutions.

You will design the electrical systems for automated machines and lines, coordinating external suppliers and supporting each project through to testing and start-up.

The role offers the opportunity to work in a technologically advanced environment, playing a hands-on part in developing and standardising the company’s electrical solutions.
