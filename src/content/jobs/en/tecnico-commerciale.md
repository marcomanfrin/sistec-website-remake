We are looking for a Sales Technician for our Sales & Business Development team, with the aim of developing new business opportunities in industrial automation and robotics.

You will promote and sell innovative systems and solutions, supporting customers from needs analysis through to after-sales management, and you will help develop service, maintenance and upgrade offerings for existing installations.

We are looking for a dynamic, results-driven person who is motivated to grow in a constantly evolving technological environment.
