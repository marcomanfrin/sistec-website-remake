We are looking for a Service & After-Sales Technician to join our after-sales service team, with the aim of providing customers with fast, effective, high-quality support.

You will manage technical service requests, coordinate remote and on-site support, schedule interventions and manage the necessary spare parts, working with the engineering departments on the continuous improvement of products and processes.

We are looking for an organised, solution-oriented and customer-focused person who is keen to grow in a technologically advanced industrial environment.
