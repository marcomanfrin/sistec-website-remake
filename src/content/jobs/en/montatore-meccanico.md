We are looking for a Mechanical Assembler to help build industrial automation systems and robotic cells.

You will be responsible for the mechanical assembly, installation and start-up of systems, coordinating a small assembly team on a day-to-day basis, both at our premises and at customer sites.

We are looking for someone with solid technical skills, a strong hands-on attitude and good organisational abilities, who can contribute to the success of our projects with high quality standards and on schedule.
