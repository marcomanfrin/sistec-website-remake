We are looking for a Robotics Programmer to join our Software & Automation team and develop advanced industrial automation and robotics solutions.

You will design and program robotic systems, turning process requirements into reliable, efficient software applications. You will follow the integration, testing and commissioning of systems, working with multidisciplinary teams and dealing directly with customers during start-up and training.
