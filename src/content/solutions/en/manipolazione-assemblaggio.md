faqs:
  - q: Can quality checks be integrated into the assembly process?
    a: Yes. We integrate machine vision systems and measuring sensors to carry out conformity checks (component presence, dimensions, correct insertion) directly during the assembly cycle.
  - q: Can you handle delicate components or parts with critical surface finishes?
    a: Absolutely. We design custom end-of-arm tooling (EOAT), whether mechanical, vacuum or magnetic, with scratch-proof materials and sensitive force control to ensure the component remains fully intact.
  - q: How complex is it to reconfigure the line for a new product?
    a: Modularity is our standard. Thanks to simulation software, adding a new product code requires only a minimal logic update, without redesigning the entire cell.
