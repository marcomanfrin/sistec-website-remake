faqs:
  - q: Is your software compatible with machines and robots from different brands?
    a: Yes. Our software architecture is multi-brand by design, bringing technologies from different manufacturers together in a single logical flow. You are not tied to one supplier, and you manage the entire cell or line from one harmonised, intuitive interface.
  - q: How does digital simulation reduce the risk of my investment?
    a: With the digital twin we create a virtual model identical to your real system. We test every motion, check every footprint and calculate cycle times with millimetre precision before a single physical component is made. The result is zero design errors and performance verified in advance.
  - q: Can I program new parts without stopping current production?
    a: Absolutely. With offline programming (OLP), your technicians generate and test programs for new batches directly on a PC while the robots on the shop floor keep working. Once a program is ready it is loaded instantly, cutting setup downtime almost to zero.
