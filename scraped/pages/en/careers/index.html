Why choose SISTEC?

Working at SISTEC AM means operating in a dynamic and structured environment, benefiting from the global strength of the Prima Industrie Group. We value professionalism and an approach focused on solving complex industrial challenges.

  • International Synergy: Be part of a world-leading Group, consolidating expertise and development capabilities on a global scale.
  • Applied Innovation: Actively participate in the design of integrated plants and production lines where theory becomes a measurable result.
  • Tech & Smart Environment: A lean organisation focused on digitalisation, simulation and process efficiency.
  • Consolidated Know-how: A wealth of 25 years’ experience as a System Integrator, turning ambitious goals into operational reality.

Who are we looking for?

We are looking for determined professionals and specialist technicians who share our mission: to increase performance, quality and profitability through modular robotic automation.
We are looking for individuals capable of interpreting our clients’ real production needs and translating them into flexible and competitive solutions.

Open Positions

Robotic Software Engineer

We are looking for a Robotic Software Engineer to join our Software & Automation team and contribute to the development of advanced industrial automation and robotics solutions.

The successful candidate will design and develop robotic software applications, translating process requirements into efficient and reliable automation systems. The role includes system integration, testing, commissioning, and customer training activities, working closely with multidisciplinary teams and supporting projects from concept through start-up.

Mechanical Technician

We are seeking a Mechanical Technician to join our team and support the assembly, installation, and commissioning of industrial automation systems and robotic cells.

The successful candidate will be responsible for leading day-to-day assembly activities, coordinating a small team of technicians, and ensuring projects are completed according to quality, safety, and schedule requirements.

This role offers the opportunity to work on advanced automation solutions in both workshop and customer site environments.

Job description

Electrical Design Engineer

We are looking for an Electrical Design Engineer to join our technical team and contribute to the development of innovative industrial automation and robotics solutions.
The successful candidate will be responsible for designing electrical systems for automated machines and production lines, coordinating external suppliers, and supporting project execution from design through commissioning.
This role offers the opportunity to work in a technologically advanced environment, contributing to both project development and the standardization of electrical engineering solutions across the organization.
Job description

Technical Service & After-Sales Specialist

We are looking for a Technical Service & After-Sales Specialist to join our Service & After-Sales team, providing high-quality support and ensuring customer satisfaction throughout the post-sales journey.

The successful candidate will be responsible for managing technical support requests, coordinating remote and on-site service activities, planning interventions, and overseeing spare parts requirements. Working closely with internal technical departments, the role will also contribute to the continuous improvement of products, processes, and customer service.

We are seeking a proactive, organized, and customer-oriented professional who thrives in a dynamic industrial environment and is eager to grow within a technologically advanced company.

Technical Sales Representative – Sales & Business Development

We are looking for a Technical Sales Representative to join our Sales & Business Development team, with the goal of developing new business opportunities in the industrial automation and robotics sector.

The successful candidate will be responsible for promoting and selling automation systems and innovative solutions, supporting customers from needs analysis through to post-sales management, while also contributing to the development of service activities, maintenance programs, and improvement projects for existing installations.

We are seeking a dynamic, results-driven individual who is eager to grow within a technologically advanced and continuously evolving environment.
